# Phase 18 — Business & Monetization

## Objective
Create a business model that generates revenue without compromising user trust, privacy, security, or platform policy compliance.

## Initial model

### Free
- Basic friend connectivity
- Limited sessions/usage
- Basic usage statistics

### Plus
Potential premium subscription with longer sessions, higher limits, priority features, advanced statistics, and multi-device capabilities.

### Pro
Potential power-user tier with higher limits and premium networking features.

Pricing must be determined from actual infrastructure, relay, payment, taxes, support, and acquisition costs.

## Business metrics

- DAU/MAU
- Retention
- Activation rate
- Free-to-paid conversion
- MRR
- Churn
- Revenue per user
- Relay cost per session
- Infrastructure cost per user
- Contribution margin

## Principle

Never design monetization around manipulating or intercepting unrelated application traffic. Monetize Linko's explicit service/features.

## Exit criteria

A tested pricing hypothesis exists, unit economics are positive or have a credible path to profitability, and monetization complies with applicable platform/payment rules.
