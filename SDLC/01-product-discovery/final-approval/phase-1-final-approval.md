# 2. Final Baseline

The approved Phase 1 baseline establishes that Linko is intended to:

> Enable a user who needs Internet connectivity to obtain authorized temporary connectivity from a willing Provider through a secure, consent-driven, technically supported connection.

The product must prioritize:

1. Explicit consent.
2. Security.
3. Privacy.
4. Reliability.
5. Usability.
6. Responsible resource consumption.
7. Sustainable economics.

---

# 3. Important Non-Claims

Phase 1 does **not** claim that:

- Connectivity will work across every carrier or network.
- Direct peer-to-peer connectivity will always be possible.
- Relay infrastructure will be economically viable at scale.
- Users will automatically adopt the product.
- Monetization is already proven.
- Google Play approval is guaranteed.

These remain validation items for later phases.

---

# 4. Approved Change-Control Principle

After Phase 1 is finally approved:

- The Phase 1 baseline becomes the authoritative discovery baseline.
- Later engineering must trace decisions back to this baseline.
- Changes must be documented rather than silently replacing requirements.
- Material changes require explicit project-owner authorization.
- New categories must be placed in the correct SDLC folder/subfolder.
- No approved decision may be casually rewritten by another AI or collaborator.
