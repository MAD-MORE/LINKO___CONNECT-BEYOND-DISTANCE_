# Phase 1.7 — Value Proposition

## Status

**COMPLETE — PROJECT-OWNER APPROVED**

## Core value proposition

> **Linko lets trusted people share Internet connectivity beyond physical distance, with explicit consent, secure session control, and a simple mobile experience.**

### Short version

> **Need Internet? Connect with someone you trust — beyond distance.**

### Brand promise

> **Connect Beyond Distance.**

## Receiver value

A Receiver can request temporary connectivity from an authorized Provider who may be far away, subject to technical/network limitations.

## Provider value

A Provider can help someone trusted remotely while retaining explicit control over approval, limits, monitoring, and termination.

## Core differentiation

1. Distance independence
2. Trusted relationships
3. Explicit consent
4. Session control
5. Mobile-first experience
6. Potential global reach where networks and regulations permit

## Positioning

> **A secure, consent-driven way for trusted people to share connectivity beyond physical distance.**

Linko must not be positioned as a magic Internet generator, carrier-bypass tool, guaranteed network-restriction workaround, or anonymous bandwidth marketplace by default.

## Core proof requirements

Linko must prove technical feasibility, acceptable reliability, Provider willingness, Receiver demand, manageable Provider resource consumption, sustainable relay economics, and viable business economics.

## Validation experiments

- Technical remote-connection testing
- User demand interviews
- Provider willingness testing
- Receiver willingness testing
- Infrastructure-cost testing

## Key risks

- Network/device limitations
- Provider reluctance to share data
- Relay costs
- Security concerns
- Carrier/legal restrictions
