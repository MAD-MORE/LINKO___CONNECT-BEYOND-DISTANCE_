# Phase 2.17 — Requirements Verification & Acceptance Criteria

## Requirements

- VER-001 P0 — Every requirement shall have an objective verification method: test, inspection, analysis, demonstration, or review.
- VER-002 P0 — Verification shall test both normal and failure paths for critical requirements.
- VER-003 P0 — Security requirements shall include adversarial testing where applicable.
- VER-004 P0 — Privacy requirements shall include data-flow and disclosure review.
- VER-005 P0 — Networking requirements shall be tested across realistic mobile-network conditions.
- VER-006 P0 — Android requirements shall be verified on supported OS/device configurations.
- VER-007 P0 — Performance requirements shall use repeatable benchmarks.
- VER-008 P0 — Reliability requirements shall include controlled failure/recovery tests.
- VER-009 P0 — Abuse requirements shall include attack simulation without harming third parties.
- VER-010 P0 — Acceptance evidence shall be stored or linked to the relevant requirement.
- VER-011 P0 — Failed acceptance criteria shall block completion of the affected requirement unless an explicit exception is approved.
- VER-012 P1 — Automated regression tests should cover stable, repeatable requirements.

## Acceptance Gate
A requirement is accepted only when its defined verification evidence passes, known exceptions are documented, and the requirement remains consistent with approved product scope.
