# Phase 2.14 — Business & Monetization Requirements

## Requirements

- BUS-001 P0 — Core connectivity must have a clearly defined user-value proposition before monetization.
- BUS-002 P0 — Any paid feature shall have an explicit entitlement model.
- BUS-003 P0 — Pricing shall be independent of security authorization decisions.
- BUS-004 P0 — Usage measurement used for billing shall be authoritative and tamper-resistant.
- BUS-005 P0 — Users shall see applicable prices, limits, and renewal conditions before purchase.
- BUS-006 P0 — Failed payments shall not silently corrupt connectivity authorization state.
- BUS-007 P0 — Refund, cancellation, and entitlement-revocation behavior shall be defined.
- BUS-008 P0 — Monetization shall not require collection of unnecessary traffic content.
- BUS-009 P1 — Free-tier limits, if used, shall be measurable and enforceable.
- BUS-010 P0 — Provider and Receiver benefits shall be clearly distinguished where plans differ.
- BUS-011 P0 — Financial records shall be auditable without exposing unnecessary personal data.
- BUS-012 P0 — Business controls shall not weaken Provider consent or user privacy.
- BUS-013 P1 — Promotions and referral systems shall have anti-fraud controls.
- BUS-014 P0 — Monetization dependencies shall have documented failure behavior.

## Acceptance
Define MVP pricing assumptions, entitlements, usage accounting, payment states, refunds, limits, and business KPIs before implementation.
