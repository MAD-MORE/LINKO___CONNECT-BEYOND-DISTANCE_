# Phase 2.15 — Compliance & Store Requirements

## Requirements

- COM-001 P0 — Linko shall identify privacy, consumer, telecommunications, platform, and security obligations applicable to each launch market.
- COM-002 P0 — Required privacy disclosures shall be available before relevant data processing.
- COM-003 P0 — User consent flows shall be documented and auditable where required.
- COM-004 P0 — Data retention and deletion policies shall map to applicable obligations.
- COM-005 P0 — Third-party processors shall be inventoried and reviewed.
- COM-006 P0 — Android permissions shall be requested only for legitimate product functions.
- COM-007 P0 — Background networking behavior shall comply with Android/Google Play requirements applicable at launch.
- COM-008 P0 — The product shall not misrepresent its networking capabilities or bypass platform/carrier restrictions unlawfully.
- COM-009 P0 — Store listing claims shall match actual product behavior.
- COM-010 P0 — Required account deletion/privacy controls shall be implemented where applicable.
- COM-011 P0 — Security and privacy disclosures shall remain consistent with implementation.
- COM-012 P0 — Legal review shall occur before public launch in each target jurisdiction.
- COM-013 P1 — Regional feature restrictions shall be supported where law or platform policy requires them.

## Acceptance
Create a jurisdiction/store checklist, privacy-policy mapping, permission review, data-processing inventory, and launch legal sign-off process.
