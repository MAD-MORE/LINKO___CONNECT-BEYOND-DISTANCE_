# Phase 19 — Linko Economy

## Objective
Explore a sustainable connectivity-sharing economy after the core networking product is proven.

## Long-term concept

A Provider may eventually earn Linko credits for verified connectivity contribution, and credits may be used for eligible Linko services.

```text
Provider shares connectivity
        ↓
Contribution recorded
        ↓
Eligible credits/rewards
        ↓
Credits used for Linko services
```

## Important safeguards

- Do not launch a rewards economy before unit economics are understood.
- Prevent farming, botting, collusion, and fake usage.
- Define expiration/transfer rules.
- Account for taxes and financial/legal obligations where applicable.
- Keep the system optional and transparent.

## Exit criteria

A formal economic model proves that rewards can be funded sustainably and safely before implementation.
