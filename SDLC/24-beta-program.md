# Phase 24 — Beta Program

## Objective
Validate Linko with real users before broad public launch.

## Stages

### Alpha
Founder-controlled testing with a few trusted devices.

### Closed beta
Approximately 10–20 users with diverse devices and networks.

### UCC/Ghana beta
Approximately 50–1,000 users depending on reliability and infrastructure capacity.

### International beta
Selected countries and carriers after Ghanaian testing is stable.

## Feedback

Collect connection failures, device compatibility, usability issues, battery impact, latency, relay cost, abuse reports, and feature requests.

## Rollout rule

Expand only when reliability, security, support, and infrastructure cost remain within defined thresholds.

## Exit criteria

Beta KPIs meet targets, critical issues are resolved, support processes exist, and the product is ready for controlled production growth.
