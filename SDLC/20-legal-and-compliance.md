# Phase 20 — Legal & Compliance

## Objective
Prepare Linko for responsible operation in Ghana and international markets.

## Required workstreams

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Account deletion
- Data retention policy
- Security contact/reporting
- Abuse reporting
- Carrier/ISP terms review
- Applicable Ghana privacy/legal review
- International privacy requirements as markets expand
- Android/Google Play policy compliance
- Payment/tax requirements

## VPN-specific consideration

Because Linko uses Android VPN functionality, its product design, disclosures, permissions, data handling, and store listing must comply with applicable Android and Google Play requirements.

## Exit criteria

Legal/compliance review is completed for the intended launch market, required policies are published, and launch-blocking platform requirements are satisfied.
